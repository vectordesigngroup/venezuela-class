Timeline: Venezuela's story

1821
Simón Bolívar wins the Battle of Carabobo. Venezuela is free from Spain.
1854
A law ends slavery in Venezuela.
1958
The last dictator leaves. Venezuela becomes a democracy.
1999
Hugo Chávez becomes president and starts socialism.
2013
Nicolás Maduro becomes president. Prices go up fast and food is hard to find.
