Timeline: Venezuela's story

1810 to 1821
Independence war led by Simón Bolívar. Battle of Carabobo, 1821. Gran Colombia is formed.
1854
President José Gregorio Monagas signs the law abolishing slavery.
1958
Dictator Marcos Pérez Jiménez falls (January). Democracy and free elections begin.
1999
Hugo Chávez takes office: nationalizations, price controls, close ties with Cuba.
2013
Nicolás Maduro takes over. Oil output falls about 70% by 2023, hyperinflation, shortages.
Nearly 8 million
Venezuelans have left the country (UN refugee agency). Many went to Colombia, Peru, Brazil, Chile and the U.S.
January 3, 2026
U.S. forces capture Maduro. He is charged in New York with narco-terrorism conspiracy and pleads not guilty.
October 25, 2026
María Corina Machado (Nobel Peace Prize 2025) returns to Venezuela. Elections are expected in 2027; no date is set.
Sources: UN refugee agency (UNHCR/R4V), U.S. Energy Information Administration, World Bank, Reuters, AP, U.S. Justice Department.
